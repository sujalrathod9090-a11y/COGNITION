            About

            Meetings shouldn't need a translator.

            MeetingMind AI was built on a simple observation: meetings generate messy notes or transcripts, and important action items often get lost or forgotten. Someone has to reread the whole thing, guess who owns what, and chase down deadlines that were only ever mentioned in passing.

            MeetingMind reads the mess for you — surfacing decisions, owners, deadlines, open questions, and risks in a format your team can act on immediately.
